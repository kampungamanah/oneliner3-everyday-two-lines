A make.fun entry for [Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479). The brief: explain one everyday thing to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence built on an analogy, then spend one more sentence saying why that analogy works — what it maps, and what it leaves out.

## The two lines

A zipper, pictured as two rows of tiny teeth holding hands one by one while a little metal runner slides along and pulls them together. Then the honest accounting: the picture covers the teeth locking in a row and the runner joining them, but it stops short of the truth about teeth — real ones bite and chew, zipper ones only grip cloth and stay shut until the runner comes back.

## Usage

Run `node main.ts` and the program writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (two lines, no trailing newline, under the 400-character gate).
